About

Analytical precision, meet creative problem-solving.

I'm a research data scientist with a PhD in materials science, on hyper-crosslinked polymers in soft matter. I lectured Environmental Engineering and sustainability at the University of Edinburgh, and I bring that grounding in several scientific and engineering disciplines to my work in data science.

Today I focus on AI: predictive modelling, large language models and the software around them. I care about high-quality code that solves real problems, and I enjoy combining analytical rigour with creative problem-solving to push what's possible.

Case study · RAG over video

Q&AI

Ask a public figure a question and the app plays the exact moment in a real interview where they answer it. Retrieval-augmented generation over 371 videos (284 hours) in English and Greek, with an evaluation harness behind every change: clips now start within a second 99% of the time, and searches take about 1.3 s.
