About

Analytical precision, meet creative problem-solving.

I lead AI engineering work at Kyndryl, where I've built core pieces of an enterprise agentic framework in pure Python (function calling, orchestration, MCP servers with a registry and gateway, agent memory and full observability), and now lead Policy as Code: turning rules extracted from business documents into Rego policies that Open Policy Agent enforces on every agent decision.

Before Kyndryl I trained and productionised text and multimodal LLMs for trade-finance compliance at Traydstream, and built LLM and RAG systems at Twyn. My background is scientific: a PhD in materials chemistry, a first-class BSc, and a year lecturing engineering at the University of Edinburgh, which is where I learnt to care about rigorous evaluation and well-made tools.
